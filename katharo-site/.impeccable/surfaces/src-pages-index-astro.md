# Surface: Katharo Cleaning landing page (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: private clients (homes, apartments) and businesses (offices, warehouses, other spaces) deciding whether to trust Katharo inside their space. Action: request a fixed-price quote. Proof on hand: four confirmed facts only (fixed written price, same team each visit, invoice/contract, products safe for children and pets). No testimonials, numbers, location or contact details exist; none may be invented. Imagery is generated atmosphere, never presented as client work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Katharo as the caretaker of a fine interwar Bucharest building: the page is the building's lobby, kept immaculate. It refuses the category default of a bright stock-photo hero with sparkle icons and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Brand colourways only, pinned by the owner: black lacquer with polished gold (the logo's gold, #CDA45E family) as the dark world, and warm cream with bronze (#F4EDE2 / #85602C) as the light world. No other hues. Interwar Bucharest lobby materials rendered in that palette: black lacquer walls, gold brass trim and lettering, cream terrazzo with bronze and black chips, geometric Art Deco ironwork, frosted glass. Brand gold K logo set like the building's brass name over the entrance. Type: a geometric Deco display face for building lettering, a quiet sans for reading.

STORY: The visitor walks into a spotless lobby and reads the tenant directory: every kind of space Katharo cleans is listed like a tenant on its floor. They learn the house rules (fixed written price, same team, invoice, safe products) from the brass rules plaque, and leave a request at the concierge desk.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The lobby tenant directory board (brass frame, dark felt, set letters) is the hero object and the navigation: rows for Locuințe, Apartamente, Birouri, Depozite, După renovare, each linking to its service. Above it, the KATHARO brass building name. Beside it, a photographic plate of an immaculate interwar lobby. The primary action, a quote starter (type of space + m² + "Cere oferta fixă"), sits at the concierge desk at the foot of the first viewport.
