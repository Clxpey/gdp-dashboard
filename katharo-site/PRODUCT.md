# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (framework chosen by the owner), building to static HTML. Deploy target not yet chosen (Netlify, Vercel or similar static host).

## Users

Two audiences, both confirmed:
- B2C: private clients who want their home or apartment cleaned by people they can trust inside it.
- B2B: businesses that need offices, warehouses and other commercial spaces cleaned, usually on a recurring contract with invoices.

Both arrive needing to decide whether this company is trustworthy enough to let in, and then ask for a quote.

## Product Purpose

Katharo Cleaning is a cleaning company. The site's job is to make a visitor trust the company and request a quote. Success is a submitted quote request.

## Positioning

Premium, discreet, consistent cleaning for homes and businesses. Confirmed differentiators:
- Fixed price agreed in writing before the job; it does not change on the day.
- The same team on every visit for recurring clients.
- Invoice and contract for every client, private or business.
- Products safe for children and pets.

## Operating Context

Clients request a quote, receive a fixed written price, then book. Recurring clients get the same team. Cleaning covers homes, apartments, offices, warehouses and other spaces ("anywhere you need it").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site language: Romanian.
- Service area: NOT confirmed. Do not name a city or region.
- Contact details (phone, email, address, company registration): NOT provided. Do not invent them.
- Quote form needs a real backend endpoint before launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Katharo Cleaning. From Greek katharós, "clean, pure".
- Logo: gold serif K with a four-point sparkle, KATHARO / CLEANING wordmark (files in ../katharo-logo/). Dark and light (cream/bronze) colourways exist.
- Reference: the owner's photo of a backlit gold sign on a dark wall with an olive branch, and a site header with "Solicită o ofertă" and "Profesionalism și încredere".

## Evidence on Hand

- No real photos of work, team or premises yet. Generated imagery may show spaces and atmosphere, but must never be presented as real client work, real staff or before/after proof.
- No testimonials, client logos, reviews, numbers, years in business or certifications. Do not fabricate any.
- NOT offered: a free-redo / satisfaction guarantee. Do not claim one.
- Unconfirmed, do not state: HEPA equipment, key handling, staff employment status or vetting process, response times.

## Product Principles

1. Trust is earned with specific, checkable promises, never with vague superlatives.
2. Only state what the company actually does. Fewer true claims beat many invented ones.
3. Serve homes and businesses without making either feel like an afterthought.
4. Every page leads to one action: request a quote.

Colour commitment (owner, binding): keep the brand colourways. Dark = black with logo gold; light = cream with bronze. No added hues.
